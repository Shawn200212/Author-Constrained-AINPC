The **17 September 2026** presentation update explains the research question, the restaurant setting, the author's contribution and current development limits. It adds three selected images: an editor menu-grip pose candidate, a child seating-adaptation candidate and a controlled menu-on-table development test. Each has a specific evidence boundary. Any retained version labels identify disclosure-reviewed presentation snapshots. They do not reproduce the original engineering commits, files, validation claims or release notes. Do not use the labels to infer a software build, benchmark result or research milestone.
