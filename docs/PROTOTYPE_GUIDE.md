# Prototype guide / 原型导览

[English overview](../README.md) · [中文概览](../README.zh-CN.md)

Read the overview for the research question, the author's contribution and current limits. The diagram separates model proposals, author constraints and UE execution. Recorded proposals and outcomes support review; they do not select actions or establish effectiveness.

The three prototype images are development captures: a restaurant overview, a table-conversation setting and guests with service staff in shared space. The [media inventory](public-media.json) records their source and dimensions. Debug markers and unfinished visuals remain visible. Image dates are capture metadata, not research milestones.

This presentation does not include an executable project or the operational research materials. [Method notes](methods/README.md) describe methods and design intent without data. See [Disclosure](../DISCLOSURE.md) and [Rights and credits](../RIGHTS_AND_CREDITS.md) for the boundary. The complete autonomous service flow and formal gameplay evaluation remain unfinished.
