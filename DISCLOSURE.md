# Public disclosure scope

This public-facing overview and its curated media introduce the research question, explain the separation of model proposals from engine execution, and describe the prototype's implementation context and current limits. This disclosure scope applies to the showcase materials, not to the contents or history of the private engineering repository.

The showcase includes approved prototype stills and a conceptual diagram. Structured proposals are described only by information category, not actual JSON keys, schemas, payloads or API examples. Author constraints and repair paths are explained as responsibilities, without concrete rule sets or repair-selection algorithms.

The showcase excludes the evaluation instrument, operational anchors, prompts, research data, analysis tables, manuscript, review correspondence, participant material, Unreal project source and third-party source assets.

Screenshots illustrate a prototype. They do not establish complete autonomous service, reliable recovery across all actions, or player benefits. The conceptual diagram is not a claim that every path has been implemented and validated.

The public website follows the same disclosure scope. More detailed academic material is shared separately in private correspondence and must not be added to the public showcase. A revised overview does not make the engineering repository or its history safe to publish.
