# Public disclosure scope

This public-facing overview introduces the research question, explains the restaurant setting and the author's contribution, and describes the separation of model proposals from engine execution. It presents selected development work with its current limits. This scope applies to the showcase materials, not to the contents or history of the private engineering repository.

The showcase includes selected development images and conceptual diagrams. Images distinguish editor pose candidates, pose-adaptation candidates, controlled development tests and runtime stills. A candidate or a test with a specific setup must not be presented as independently validated behavior or a complete autonomous interaction.

Structured proposals are described only by information category, not actual JSON keys, schemas, payloads or API examples. Author constraints and repair paths are explained as responsibilities, without concrete rule sets or repair-selection algorithms.

The showcase excludes evaluation instruments, operational anchors, prompts, research data, analysis tables, manuscripts, review correspondence, participant material, Unreal project source and third-party source assets. Local paths and internal test identifiers are not publication inputs.

Screenshots do not establish complete autonomous service, reliable recovery across all actions or player benefits. The conceptual diagrams do not claim that every path has been implemented and validated. Development verification does not establish independently evaluated effectiveness.

The public website follows the same disclosure scope. More detailed academic material is shared separately in private correspondence and must not be added to the public showcase. A revised overview does not make the engineering repository or its history safe to publish.
